Edex App Privacy Policy

For parents, students and teachers using the Edex mobile app (Android package pk.edex.app).

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Who we are

Edex is a school management system used by schools in Pakistan. The Edex mobile app lets parents and students see their school diary, attendance, fee and results, and lets teachers mark attendance, write the diary and enter marks.

Accounts are created by the school, not in the app. Parents and students sign in with the school code, the admission number and a password given by the school. Teachers sign in with the school code, their employee ID and a password given by the school.

The current version uses demo data only

This version of the app is a preview. It is not connected to any school's server yet.

  • Every student, teacher, fee, attendance and result shown in the app is made-up demo data that is built into the app.
  • The app does not send any personal data to Edex or to anyone else. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you type (for example a changed password, attendance or diary in the demo) stays on your phone and is reset when the app restarts.

We will update this policy, and the app's Data safety section on Google Play, before the app is connected to a real school.

Information stored on your phone

  • Sign-in session: which demo account is signed in, kept in the phone's secure storage so you stay signed in.
  • Homework ticks and settings: which homework you ticked as done, and your light or dark mode choice.

This information never leaves your phone. Signing out, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

When the app is connected to your school

Once your school starts using the Edex app with its real data, the app will show information that your school already keeps in Edex. The school decides what is entered and is responsible for it; Edex processes it on the school's behalf.

Parents and students

  • Student name, admission number, class and section, photo, and parent name and phone number
  • Attendance, school diary (homework, classwork, tests, notes), time table
  • Fee challans, payments and dues; exam marks, results and report cards

Teachers

  • Name, employee ID, classes and subjects taught, own attendance and salary slips
  • Attendance, diary entries and marks the teacher enters for their classes

This information will be sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and used only to run the school's Edex service. Passwords are stored in hashed form. We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone except service providers that host Edex for us, or where the law requires it.

Links to other apps

Buttons such as "WhatsApp" open another app (WhatsApp or the phone dialer). Edex does not see what you send there; that app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The Edex app is meant for parents and teachers. Student records are entered by the school. A student's login is given by the school to the family, and we expect a parent to supervise its use. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and the app has no ads, chat with strangers or in-app purchases.

Keeping and deleting data

In this demo version nothing is kept outside your phone. Once connected, a school keeps records for as long as it uses Edex. To correct or delete a student's or teacher's information, or to close a login, contact your school; you can also write to us at the email below and we will pass the request to the school and help it act on it.
